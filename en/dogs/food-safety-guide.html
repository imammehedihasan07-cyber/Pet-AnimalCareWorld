Foods Dogs Can and Cannot Eat: A Safety Guide

👨‍⚕️
Reviewed by: Imam Mehedi Hasan, VFA
Last Updated: August 2026

Quick Summary

Not all human food is safe for dogs. While some fruits and vegetables provide great nutrients, others can cause kidney failure, anemia, or fatal poisoning. Always consult your vet before adding new human foods to your dog's diet.

✅ Safe Human Foods

  • Plain cooked chicken/turkey
  • Carrots, green beans
  • Apples (without seeds)
  • Blueberries, bananas

❌ Toxic Foods (AVOID)

  • Grapes and raisins
  • Onions, garlic, chives
  • Chocolate and caffeine
  • Xylitol (artificial sweetener)

Why Certain Foods Are Toxic

Dogs have different metabolic pathways than humans. For example, the compound in chocolate (theobromine) is harmless to humans but toxic to dogs, as they cannot metabolize it effectively, leading to heart and nervous system issues.

Important: If your dog accidentally ingests toxic food, contact your vet immediately. Read our Medical Disclaimer.
