Litter Box Training Your Cat

Reviewed by: Imam Mehedi Hasan, VFA

Litter box training is usually instinctive for cats, but they need the right environment to succeed. Here is how to make it easy.

1. Choose the Right Location

Place the litter box in a quiet, low-traffic area where your cat feels safe and can access easily at all times.

2. Select the Right Litter

Most cats prefer unscented, clumping litter. Avoid strong odors that may deter them.

3. Routine is Key

Place your cat in the litter box after meals, naps, or play sessions to encourage them to use it.

If your cat suddenly stops using the box, check for health issues (like urinary tract infections) before assuming it's a behavioral problem.
