Why Is My Cat Vomiting?

Reviewed by: Imam Mehedi Hasan, VFA

Cat vomiting can be alarming, but it’s a common issue. It’s important to distinguish between "hairballs" and serious medical emergencies.

Common Causes

  • Dietary Indiscretion: Eating something spoiled or a sudden change in food.
  • Hairballs: Frequent grooming leads to fur accumulation.
  • Ingesting Foreign Objects: Swallowing toys, strings, or plants (highly dangerous!).
  • Systemic Diseases: Kidney failure, hyperthyroidism, or diabetes.

When to See a Vet Immediately

Emergency Signs: If your cat is vomiting multiple times in one day, lethargic, has blood in vomit, or hasn't eaten for 24+ hours, take them to a vet immediately.

If the vomiting is occasional and the cat is otherwise active and eating, you may monitor them. However, for chronic or severe vomiting, professional diagnosis is required.
