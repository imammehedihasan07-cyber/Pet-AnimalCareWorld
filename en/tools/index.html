How Are Cat Years Calculated?

Contrary to popular belief, 1 cat year is not simply equal to 7 human years. Feline development happens rapidly in the first two years of life:

  • First Year: A 1-year-old cat is roughly equal to a 15-year-old human.
  • Second Year: By age 2, a cat reaches human adulthood, equivalent to about 24 years old.
  • Each Year After: Every additional year equals approximately 4 human years.
