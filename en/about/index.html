About Pet Care World

Bridging the gap between professional veterinary field experience and everyday pet care.

Our Mission

At Pet & Animal Care World, our mission is simple: to provide pet owners with practical, evidence-informed, and highly actionable care guidelines. We understand that taking care of a pet goes beyond just feeding them—it requires understanding their unique nutritional needs, behavioral signs, and recognizing when they need medical attention.

The Expertise Behind the Guides

The foundation of Pet Care World is built on real-world animal healthcare. The platform is managed and curated by Imam Mehedi Hasan, a dedicated Veterinary Field Assistant (VFA) and livestock medical practitioner.

Operating out of Mehedi Medical Hall located in Ghosgaon Bazar, Imam Mehedi Hasan brings years of hands-on experience in animal treatment, first-aid, and medicinal inventory management directly to this platform. This practical, field-level expertise ensures that the guidelines, nutrition plans, and health warning signs discussed on this website are grounded in daily, real-world veterinary practices.

Our Editorial Promise (E-E-A-T)

In an era of automated content, we strictly adhere to Google's principles of Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness.

  • Real Experience: Insights are drawn from actual field treatments and interactions with pet owners.
  • Clear Transparency: We clearly distinguish between everyday care advice and critical veterinary emergencies.
  • No False Claims: We do not promise guaranteed medical outcomes. Our guides help you make informed decisions, but we always advocate for in-person veterinary consultations for serious health issues.
